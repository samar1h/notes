Not life hacks. Not systems. Just things I've noticed actually work — for me, right now.

## The physical stuff

**Cold water in the morning** — not for any biohacking reason, just wakes me up faster than anything else.

**Noise-cancelling headphones** — even without music. The physical act of putting them on signals something to my brain.

**Standing up when stuck** — a two-minute walk around the room does more than ten minutes of staring at the problem.

## The mental stuff

**Starting with the smallest possible piece of the task.** Not "write the report" but "open the document and type the date." Momentum is real.

**Not checking anything before I start work.** Email, messages, news — all of it can wait an hour. Whatever's there at 8am is still there at 9am.

**Writing down what I'm going to do before I do it.** Takes 30 seconds. Saves significant time from wandering.

## What doesn't work for me

- Time-blocking (too rigid, too much overhead)
- Pomodoro timers (the alarm breaks flow more than it helps)
- "Eat the frog" (I do better when I build momentum with easier things first)

The point isn't to copy someone else's system. It's to notice what's actually happening and do more of that.
