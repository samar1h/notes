This is where I keep my notes. Not a blog, not a journal — just a place to put things down before they slip away.

## What goes here

Anything worth remembering: ideas half-formed, things I've read, thoughts that arrived uninvited at 2am. The format doesn't matter. The act of writing does.

## How it works

Notes are written in Markdown and published here. Simple as that. No comments, no analytics, no social share buttons. Just the writing.

## A few ground rules I set for myself

- Write when something is worth writing, not on a schedule
- Don't optimize for an audience — write for future-me
- Shorter is almost always better
- A bad draft is better than a good silence

That's it. Let's see how this goes.
